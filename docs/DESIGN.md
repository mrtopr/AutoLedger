# AutoLedger ERP - Design System & Visual Contract

## Core Aesthetic: Modern Commercial DMS with Refined Curved Geometry
A modern, polished automotive Dealership Management System (DMS) combining enterprise precision, high information density, and modern curved geometry (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl` containers, `rounded-lg` controls, subtle 1px borders, and soft shadows).

---

## 1. Color System (Light Modern Enterprise)
- **Canvas / Background**: `#F8F9FA` (Soft neutral grey)
- **Surfaces / Cards**: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp white)
- **Border**: `#E2E8F0` (1px subtle border) / `#CBD5E1` (Input borders)
- **Primary Text**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
- **Secondary Text**: `#475569` (Slate 600)
- **Muted Text**: `#64748B` / `#94A3B8` (Slate 500/400)
- **Primary Accent**: `#C81E1E` (Enterprise Crimson)
- **Hover Crimson**: `#A81818` / `#991B1B`

---

## 2. Corner Radii & Curvature Hierarchy
Modern, smooth corner radii that feel contemporary and cohesive:
- **Card Containers & Panels**: `rounded-xl` (12px) or `rounded-2xl` (16px)
- **Modal Dialogs & Drawers**: `rounded-2xl` (16px)
- **Buttons & Action Triggers**: `rounded-lg` (8px) / `rounded-xl` (10px)
- **Input Fields & Selects**: `rounded-lg` (8px)
- **Metric Cards & Sub-panels**: `rounded-xl` (12px)
- **Pills, Filters & Tags**: `rounded-lg` (8px) or `rounded-full`
- **Table Wrappers**: `rounded-xl overflow-hidden`

---

## 3. Typography & Numerical Precision
- **Primary Body**: `Inter`, 13px base, line-height 1.5
- **Monetary & Numbers**: `JetBrains Mono` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`
- **Part Numbers & SKUs**: `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase
- **Table Headers**: 10px / 11px uppercase bold with letter-spacing `tracking-wider`

---

## 4. Spacing & Density
- Base grid: 4px / 8px / 12px / 16px / 24px
- Table row height: `py-2.5 px-3.5`
- Form controls: `h-8.5` / `h-9` with `px-3`
